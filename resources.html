Emergency and crisis resources for our patients and their families.

Emergencies

If you are having a life-threatening emergency or an urgent clinical need that cannot wait until regular business hours, please utilize the local emergency resources below:

  • Emergency Call 911 or go to your nearest emergency room
  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline Call or text 988
  • Mecklenburg County Crisis Center (704) 566-3410
  • South Carolina Department of Mental Health — 24/7 Mobile Crisis (833) 364-2274
  • Atrium Health Behavioral Health — 24/7 Call Center (704) 444-2400
